# Question-2
## How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A, and P?

B = MAP means Behaviour = Motivation + Ability + Prompt. To build habits, keep the action easy, stay motivated, and use a clear trigger.

# Question-5
## What is the book's perspective on Identity?

Atomic Habits says that real change starts with who you want to become, not just your goals. For example, instead of “lose weight,” think “be a healthy person.”

# Question-6
## Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

Atomic Habits says to remove obstacles and make habits easier to do. For example, keeping workout clothes ready can make it easier to exercise in the morning.

# Question-7
## Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

Atomic Habits suggests making bad habits harder to do. For example, uninstall social media apps or keep your phone in another room to avoid using it.
